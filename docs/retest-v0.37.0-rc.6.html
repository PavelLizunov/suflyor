suflyor v0.37.0-rc.6 - tile read-aloud player

  1. Open a long answer tile and start read-aloud. The speaker control turns into Stop; the tile's More menu exposes Back 10 seconds, Forward 15 seconds, and Reading speed.
  2. Use Back and Forward while Tera is reading. Position stays inside the synthesized/readable text; repeated Forward near the end does not crash or invent future audio.
  3. Cycle Reading speed: 1x → 1.25x → 1.5x → 2x → 1x. Speech stays pitch-preserved and a new tile always starts at 1x.
  4. Pause then resume at a non-1x speed, and Stop while speed is changed. The tile resets to idle and a later read is normal speed.
  5. Repeat start/pause/stop on Piper. Its controls may react after the current synthesis chunk, but EOS must drain and the sidecar must become idle without continuing to write silence.
  6. During a recorded session, slow playback to 0.5x through the protocol-level smoke test. STT remains suppressed while TTS plays and resumes after the bounded drain tail when paused or stopped.
  7. Before committing/releasing on Winbrat, regenerate and stage both tracked sidecar lockfiles: suflyor-tts/Cargo.lock and suflyor-teratts/Cargo.lock. This checkout deliberately does not run Cargo on the owner PC.
